# Academic Study Schedule & Examination Readiness Guidelines

## Section 1: Standard Exam Readiness Benchmark
A student is formally categorized as "Exam Ready" if and only if their completed topic coverage reaches or exceeds 80% of the prescribed curriculum. Students with coverage below 80% require active remediation and daily study hour allocation to close the deficit before the examination date.

## Section 2: Topic Complexity Tiers and Workload Estimation
Curriculum topics are divided into three standard workload tiers:
- Tier 1 (Foundational / Conceptual): Requires 1.0 study hour per topic.
- Tier 2 (Standard Applied / Analytical): Requires 1.5 to 2.0 study hours per topic.
- Tier 3 (Advanced Synthesis / Algorithmic): Requires 2.5 to 3.5 study hours per topic.
When topic difficulty is unspecified, the institutional baseline default is 2.0 study hours per topic.

## Section 3: Daily Study Load Limits and Burnout Thresholds
Cognitive retention declines rapidly after extended study sessions. Institutional guidelines enforce:
- Normal Sustainable Pace: 2.0 to 4.0 hours per day.
- Intensive Pace: 4.1 to 6.0 hours per day.
- Extreme Burnout Threshold: Any calculated schedule exceeding 6.0 hours per day triggers a high-intensity cognitive fatigue warning and mandates explicit human approval/oversight before plan finalization.
- Hard Cap: Schedules demanding more than 10.0 hours per day are deemed unsafe and rejected.

## Section 4: Emergency Cramming vs Steady Pacing Policy
If the remaining time until the exam is 2 days or fewer, and remaining topics exceed 4, the student is in the "Critical Crunch" zone. Under this condition:
- The planner must flag high risk of incomplete retention.
- Secondary elective topics should be deferred in favor of core exam objectives.
- The human supervisor must acknowledge the accelerated pace.

## Section 5: Core Topic Weightage and Priority Catalog
In the core curriculum examination:
- Module A: Search & Knowledge Representation (Weightage: 30%, Core Mandatory).
- Module B: Machine Learning & Statistical Inference (Weightage: 35%, Core Mandatory).
- Module C: Natural Language & Agent Architectures (Weightage: 20%, Core Mandatory).
- Module D: Advanced Specialized Extensions (Weightage: 15%, Elective).

## Section 6: Study Scheduling and Cognitive Rest Intervals
To maintain peak mental acuity, daily study must follow structured intervals:
- 50-minute focused study blocks paired with 10-minute restorative breaks.
- A mandatory 45-minute meal break for every continuous 4-hour block.
- Study sessions past 11:00 PM are discouraged to protect memory consolidation sleep phases.

## Section 7: Final Day Revision Buffer Policy
The final 24 hours (1 calendar day) before the official examination must be preserved exclusively for formula review, summary notes, and sleep. No new syllabus topics may be scheduled on Day 0 or the evening prior to the exam.

## Section 8: Retake Rules and Attendance Prerequisites
Students are eligible for exam sitting only if their institutional attendance is above 75%. If a student fails to meet the 80% readiness threshold before exam day, institutional remedial tutoring is offered prior to supplementary examination rounds.
